Day 1

10:30pm Prayer · Sunday

1 Peter 1:13

“[Be Holy] Therefore, with minds that are alert and fully sober, set your hope on the grace to be brought to you when Jesus Christ is revealed at his coming.”

In this passage, Peter is awakening believers from spiritual dullness. He is speaking like a shepherd who knows that faith can grow sleepy under pressure, suffering, or waiting. His message is clear: salvation is secure, but spiritual alertness is essential.

Peter is telling believers to wake up not physically, but spiritually. More specifically â€” do not sleep spiritually. Stay awake, think clearly, live intentionally, and fix your hope completely on Christ. Those who remain sober in spirit will recognize His work and walk in holiness.

Prayer: Lord Yeshua, awaken our spirits. Remove laziness, doubt, and distraction from our minds. Help us to stay sober and alert, fixing our hope fully on Christ. As You reveal Yourself to us, strengthen our faith to walk in holiness and obedience. Amen.
